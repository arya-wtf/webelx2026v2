# 11 Who We're Not For

## Headline
We are not for everyone. Built for a few.

## Supporting Copy
We do our best work with teams that have a real product problem, a clear owner, and the urgency to move. If that sounds like you, we will probably get along.

## We Don't Take On
- Just a quick logo and a banner. Aside: Quick is a budget word.
- Make it look like Apple. Aside: Apple has 160K employees. You have a runway.
- A 6-month spec doc, then build. Aside: You'd be out of cash before kickoff.
- Pixel-perfect or it is wrong. Aside: Pixels are not the product. Outcomes are.
- "Use AI" as the entire brief. Aside: AI is a tool. Founders are the strategy.

## We're Built For
- Teams that know how decisions get made.
- MVPs, SaaS products, and digital products that need to launch.
- Briefs that start with the user problem.
- Teams that want product design and build connected.
- Partners who want measurable product progress.
