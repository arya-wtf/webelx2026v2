## Stages

### Seed
Headline: YOUR FIRST VERSION. SHIPPED BEFORE IT BECOMES YOUR NEXT YEAR.

Copy: Typical sprint: 2 to 4 weeks. You've got the idea. Maybe a deck. Maybe a doc. Maybe just a voice note from a call that went well. That's enough to start. Two to four weeks later, you have a real product to show — not a prototype, not a concept. Something you can put in front of users.

CTA: START A SEED SPRINT

Services:
- 01 Clarity on what to actually build first | Your goals, constraints, and user context get structured into a scope that a team can act on — not a 40-slide deck that nobody reads twice.
- 02 User flows that make sense from day one | The key screens, decision points, and product logic get mapped so your first users don't need a tutorial to get the value.
- 03 An interface that looks like you meant it | Visual direction, UI design, and landing page story come together so the first version earns trust — not just attention.
- 04 Built and ready to ship | Webflow, Framer, no-code, or custom code — whichever gets your product live faster without creating technical debt you'll regret in six months.

### Growth
Headline: YOUR PRODUCT IS LIVE. USERS AREN'T STAYING. LET'S FIX THAT.

Copy: Typical sprint: 3 to 6 weeks. Something in the product is losing people. Onboarding, activation, a dashboard that's technically complete but functionally confusing. You know the drop-off is there — you just need someone to find it, fix it, and ship the improvement before another quarter passes.

CTA: START A GROWTH SPRINT

Services:
- 01 A diagnosis, not a guess | Friction across onboarding, activation, key flows, and dashboards gets identified systematically — not based on a hunch or one loud customer complaint.
- 02 Flows that actually move users forward | The structure, content logic, and interaction decisions get redesigned so users get to the value without hitting a wall.
- 03 Key screens rebuilt around what users need | Onboarding, activation, dashboards, or conversion-critical areas — redesigned with intent, not just cleaned up cosmetically.
- 04 Specs your team can ship from | Every redesign decision gets documented into handoff-ready assets. No translation layer. No "what did they mean by this?"

### Scale
Headline: YOUR TEAM IS GROWING. DESIGN SHOULDN'T SLOW THEM DOWN.

Copy: Ongoing. Every new feature takes longer than it should because nobody can find the right component. Every new hire spends two weeks figuring out "how we do things here." A scalable design system fixes both — and makes everything your team ships look like it belongs together.

CTA: START A SCALE ENGAGEMENT

Services:
- 01 A system built around how your product actually works | Not a generic component library dropped on your repo. Your screens, patterns, and recurring decisions get mapped into a structure that fits the product you have — and the one you're building toward.
- 02 Components your team will actually use | Reusable UI pieces with clear usage rules, edge case coverage, and documentation that doesn't require a 90-minute onboarding session to understand.
- 03 Design and build that move together | Product design decisions connect directly to implementation — across Webflow, Framer, no-code, or custom frontend — so there's no spec translation lag between what's designed and what ships.
- 04 AI workflows where they actually add speed | AI-assisted tooling integrated into the places where it removes friction — not bolted on to look modern.
